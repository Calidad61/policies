# Privacy Policy for Little Artists Studio

**Last Updated:** October 25, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 25, 2026

Welcome to **Little Artists Studio** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and safety of all our users, especially young children and their families.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information in compliance with the **Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**, the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K)**, and the **Google Play Families Policy**.

---

## 1. Summary of Our Child Safety Commitment

- 🛡️ **Zero Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information.
- 🚫 **No Advertisements:** The app contains absolutely zero third-party ads or pop-ups.
- 📴 **100% Offline Capability:** The app functions entirely on your device without requiring an active internet connection.
- 🎨 **Local Artwork Storage:** All drawings, colorings, and creations stay on your device and are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud.
- 🔒 **Parental Gate:** Settings and sensitive controls are protected behind a math-based parental gate to ensure children cannot access them accidentally.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe children's creative apps should be safe and private. We do **not** collect, solicit, or share:
- Names, physical addresses, or phone numbers
- Email addresses or user account credentials
- Precise or coarse location data (GPS, IP geolocation)
- Photos, videos, or camera access
- Voice recordings or microphone access
- Contacts, SMS, or social media accounts
- Persistent device identifiers (IMEI, Advertising ID, MAC address)
- Biometric or health information

No account creation, login, or registration is ever required to play.

---

## 3. How Artwork & App Data are Handled

- **Artwork & Projects:** Any drawing, doodle, coloring page, or connected-dot artwork created within Little Artists Studio is saved **exclusively in the application's local sandbox storage** on your device.
- **Preferences & Settings:** Simple user preferences (such as sound effects volume and theme preferences) are stored locally on your device via standard local storage (`SharedPreferences` and local SQLite database).
- **No Cloud Synchronization:** None of this data is transmitted, synchronized, or backed up to external servers by our app.
- **Data Deletion:** You can delete any saved artwork at any time directly through the in-app Art Gallery, or delete all app data by uninstalling the application or clearing the app storage in your device settings.

---

## 4. Device Permissions

Little Artists Studio requests only the minimal permissions required for basic app functionality:
- **Internal Storage (App-Specific):** Used strictly to save and load your child's artwork files and thumbnails locally on the device.
- **Haptic / Vibration Feedback:** Used to provide gentle tactile feedback during drawing and button taps.

The app does **not** request access to the Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, or Phone State.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services, Ads, and Tracking

- **No Third-Party Advertising:** We do not use any third-party ad networks (such as AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.). Children will never see advertisements while using Little Artists Studio.
- **No Behavioral Analytics or Tracking:** We do not employ third-party analytics SDKs (such as Facebook SDK, Firebase Analytics, or adjust) that track user behavior across apps or websites.
- **No Social Media Integration:** The app does not connect to any social media platforms.

---

## 6. Sharing Artwork (Parent-Initiated Only)

Little Artists Studio provides an optional "Share" feature that allows an artwork image to be shared using the operating system's standard system share sheet. 
- This feature is strictly optional and user-initiated.
- We do not transmit or view the image being shared. The operating system handles sending the file to the app chosen by the user (such as printing or messaging an artwork to a family member).

---

## 7. Compliance with Children's Privacy Laws

### Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
We comply fully with COPPA. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that personal data has inadvertently been collected, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete it.

### GDPR / GDPR-K (European Union & UK)
In accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (and the UK GDPR), we do not process personal data of children. As no personal data is collected or stored on remote servers, no profiling, automated decision-making, or cross-border data transfers occur.

### Google Play Families Policy
Little Artists Studio adheres to all requirements under Google Play's Families Policy, including strict ad-free operation, no transmission of personal identifiers, and inclusion of parental gates where appropriate.

---

## 8. Parental Controls & Grown-Up Gate

To ensure that only adults access application configuration, we incorporate a **Parental Gate** (requiring the resolution of a randomized math challenge) before accessing:
- Parent Settings
- App Feedback / Contact links

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or app features. Any updates will be posted to this document with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage parents and guardians to review this policy periodically.
