# Cric Scorer - Data Deletion Instructions

Google Play requires that we provide a way for users to delete their account and data. You can do this at any time through one of the following methods:

### Method 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)
1. Open the **Cric Scorer** app on your device.
2. Navigate to the **Profile** tab.
3. Scroll down to the **Danger Zone** section at the bottom.
4. Tap **Delete Account**.
5. Read the warning and tap **DELETE FOREVER** to confirm. 

---

### Method 2: Delete via Web/Email Request
If you have already uninstalled the app and wish to have your data removed from our servers, please follow these steps:

1. Send an email to: **calidaddeveloper@gmail.com**
2. Include the subject: **"Account Deletion Request"**
3. In the email, please provide the registered email address or phone number you used to sign in to Cric Scorer.
4. Our support team will process your request and permanently remove your user profile and all cloud-synced matches within **7 days**.

---

### What Data will be Deleted?
When your request is processed:
- Your **Personal Profile** (Name, Email, UID) is permanently deleted.
- All **Cricket Match Data** synced to our Firebase cloud is removed.
- All **Tournament Data** created by your account is removed.

*Please note: This action is irreversible. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered even if you sign back in.*
