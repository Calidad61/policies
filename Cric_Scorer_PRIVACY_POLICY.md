# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: April 15, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Cric Scorer** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information
When you use certain features of the App, such as creating an account or syncing data, we may collect the following information:
- **Identifier Information:** Your name, email address, and profile picture (when signing in via Google or creating a Firebase account).
- **Authentication Data:** We use Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication to verify your identity and secure your account.

### 1.2 App Data
We collect information related to the core functionality of the App:
- **Cricket Match Data:** Player names, team names, match scores, overs, balls, and tournament details. This data is stored locally on your device and, if you are signed in, synchronized with our secure cloud servers (Firebase/Cloud Firestore).

### 1.3 Usage and Technical Data
- **Device Information:** We may collect information about the mobile device you use, including the hardware model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
- **Log Data:** We may collect information that your browser or device sends whenever you use our App.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- **Provide and Maintain the App:** To process match scores and manage tournament data.
- **Account Management:** To manage your user account and provide synchronization features across devices.
- **Improve the App:** To understand usage patterns and enhance user experience.
- **Communication:** To send you important updates or respond to your inquiries.

---

## 3. Storage and Data Security

- **Local Storage:** Some data is stored locally on your device using a secure SQLite database (Drift).
- **Cloud Storage:** If you enable cloud synchronization, your data is stored securely using **Google Firebase** (Cloud Firestore, Authentication, and Realtime Database).
- **Security:** We prioritize the security of your data. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 6. Data Deletion and Account Removal

We respect your right to control your personal data. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time through the following methods:

- **In-App Deletion:** Navigate to the **Profile** tab, scroll to the **Danger Zone**, and select **Delete Account**. This will permanently remove your user profile and all cloud-synced match data.
- **Request via Email:** If you can no longer access the app, you may request account deletion by emailing us at **[Your Contact Email Here]**. Please include your registered email address. We will process your request within 7 days.
